WARRIOR PROFILE

Welcome to my realm. I am a 3D animator and digital creator dedicated to pushing the technical boundaries of spatial movement, keyframe manipulation, and physical prop interactions.

This portfolio represents a relentless journey through digital design, blending tactical choreography with arcade-inspired, retro responsive environments. Every frame, lighting profile, and muscle micro-reaction within these projects was handcrafted from scratch to deliver high-impact visual storytelling.

Whether executing dynamic aerial physics setups or technical first-person weapon manipulation loops, my mission remains the same: total domination over the digital craft.

ARSENAL: Source 2 Filmmaker (S2FM), Blender, Premiere Pro
SPECIALTIES: 3D Motion, Weight Dynamics, Tactical Realism
